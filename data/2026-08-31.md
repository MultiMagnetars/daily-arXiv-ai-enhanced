### [1] [Magnetospheric birefringence breaks the rotating-vector model and depolarises X-ray pulsars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27699)
*Alexander A. Mushtukov,Sergey S. Tsygankov,Silvia Zane,Nabil Brice,Ruth M. E. Kelly,Roberto Taverna,Roberto Turolla*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 本文表明，吸积X射线脉冲星磁层中的传播效应可通过QED双折射区域内的非绝热轨迹和等离子体加载吸积流中的附加相位位移，同时产生偏振角的系统性偏离和线偏振度降低，从而可能解释观测到的低偏振分数和异常角度摆动。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准图像预期吸积X射线脉冲星在回旋共振以下表现出大幅线性偏振，且偏振角遵循旋转矢量模型。然而，近期的X射线偏振观测显示偏振分数适中，位置角摆动常偏离这一简单图像，构成需要解释的知识缺口。

Method: 摘要未说明具体观测或仪器，明确的是利用广义相对论光线追踪（ray tracing）和斯托克斯参数输运（Stokes-parameter transport）进行磁层传播的建模，分析从致密极区发出的光子在QED双折射区域和等离子体加载吸积流中的传播。

Result: 光子通过磁层传播以两种方式获得依赖光线的偏振角偏移：一是近乎沿磁场方向的轨迹穿过QED双折射区域内的局部非绝热包层；二是与等离子体加载吸积流相交的光子在等离子体简正模间累积额外相位位移。两种效应加宽偏振角分布并降低热点积分线偏振度，同时产生偏离旋转矢量模型的系统性偏差。

Conclusion: 作者认为磁层传播应被视为吸积X射线脉冲星偏振转移问题的一部分，并可能有助于解释观测到的低偏振分数和异常角度摆动。该结论基于建模工作，提出了一种物理解释；其与脉冲星或磁星观测的相关性仅停留在可能性层面，摘要未明确建立。

Abstract: Accreting X-ray pulsars have long been expected to show large linear polarisation below the cyclotron resonance. In the standard picture, this follows from two facts: radiative opacities in the strongly magnetised neutron star environment depend strongly on polarisation mode, and the emission comes from compact polar regions. The emerging radiation should therefore be locally polarised and add almost coherently, with a polarisation angle tracing the projected magnetic field as in the rotating-vector model. Recent X-ray polarimetric observations, instead, show modest polarisation fractions and position angle swings that often depart from this simple picture. Here we show that propagation through the magnetosphere of an accreting neutron star can naturally produce both effects. Using general-relativistic ray tracing and Stokes-parameter transport, we find that photons emitted from compact polar regions acquire ray-dependent polarisation angle offsets in two ways. First, nearly field-aligned trajectories can cross local non-adiabatic pockets inside the QED birefringent region. Second, photons that intersect the plasma-loaded accretion flow accumulate additional phase shifts between plasma normal modes. Both effects broaden the distribution of polarisation angles across the ray bundle and reduce the hotspot-integrated linear polarisation, while also producing systematic deviations from rotating-vector-model expectations. Magnetospheric propagation should therefore be treated as part of the polarimetric transfer problem in accreting X-ray pulsars, and may help explain the observed low polarisation fractions and anomalous angle swings.

### [2] [Distinct Modes of Pulsar Glitch Activity Revealed by the Waiting-Time--Amplitude Morphology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28523)
*Wen-Tong Liu,Wei-hua Wang,Xia Zhou,Fei-fei Kou*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该研究基于349次自转突变样本的等待时间—幅度联合分布，提出客观分类方案，将脉冲星自转突变划分为Compact与Extended两类形态，并识别出平均后向等待时间为最强类别预测因子，且两类分别对应近乎完全储层耗竭与部分雪崩式释放。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统上脉冲星自转突变被定性分为"Vela-like"和"Crab-like"，但这些定性类别缺乏可复现的定量边界。本文旨在构建基于等待时间与自转突变幅度联合分布的客观分类方案，以提供可定量、不依赖视觉检查的划分标准。

Method: 交叉比对Jodrell Bank与ATNF自转突变数据库，汇编22颗脉冲星的349次自转突变样本；使用二维核密度估计（KDE）结合最高密度区域（HDR）导出面积、各向异性比和HDR外占比等几何描述量；对描述量进行层次聚类，并辅以自助重采样和超参数敏感性检验；采用逻辑回归识别类别预测因子（含$\mathrm{AUC}$和$\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{CV}}$），并进行了基于$Δt_{+}$的平行分析。

Result: 层次聚类识别出Compact与Extended两个形态类别：Compact类具有较小HDR面积、较高各向异性和有限边缘占有率；Extended类具有更宽HDR占有率和更强低密度延伸。绝大多数源的划分得到自助重采样和超参数检验确认，但PSR J0537-6910与PSR J1341-6220处于中间位置。逻辑回归表明平均后向等待时间$\langleΔt_{-}\rangle$最强（样本内$\mathrm{AUC}=0.88$；留一法$\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{CV}}=0.59$）；$Δt_{+}$平行分析复现两类结构但稳定性降低。

Conclusion: 作者认为Compact类与近乎完全的储层耗竭一致，而Extended类反映部分、雪崩式的释放；因此对Extended类脉冲星中$G$的短期测量可能低估真实的壳层超流占比。摘要中未涉及脉冲星计时、磁星或FRB等直接关联，故不对此作出判断。根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: Pulsar glitches display diverse behaviors. They are commonly labeled as "Vela-like" or "Crab-like," yet these qualitative categories do not provide reproducible quantitative boundaries. We constructed an objective classification scheme based on the joint distribution of backward waiting time $Δt_{-}$ and fractional glitch amplitude $Δν/ν$. A sample of 349 glitches from 22 pulsars was compiled by cross-matching the Jodrell Bank and ATNF glitch databases. For each source, we used two-dimensional kernel density estimation (KDE) together with highest-density regions (HDRs) to derive geometric descriptors, including the HDR area, anisotropy ratio, and out-of-HDR fraction. Hierarchical clustering of the KDE--HDR descriptors revealed two morphology classes independent of visual inspection. In this convention, the Compact class is associated with small HDR areas, elevated anisotropy, and limited peripheral occupancy. The Extended class is associated with broader HDR occupancy and stronger low-density extensions. We found that bootstrap resampling and hyperparameter sensitivity tests confirmed this partition for most sources. Nevertheless, two prolific pulsars (PSR J0537-6910 and PSR J1341-6220) were found to occupy intermediate positions. Logistic regression identified the mean backward waiting time $\langleΔt_{-}\rangle$ as the strongest class predictor (in-sample $\mathrm{AUC}=0.88$; leave-one-out $\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{CV}}=0.59$, reflecting the limited sample size). Additionally, a parallel analysis using $Δt_{+}$ recovered the same two-class structure but with reduced stability. The data suggest that the Compact class is consistent with near-complete reservoir depletion, whereas the Extended class reflects partial, avalanche-like releases. As a result, short-term measurements of $G$ in Extended-class pulsars may underestimate the true crustal superfluid fraction.

### [3] [High Dispersion Measure Fast Radio Bursts from Young, Dust-Poor H II Regions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27779)
*Diggins, Eliza C.,Leung, Calvin,Lu, Wenbin*

Main category: scix

TL;DR: 作者通过半解析模型和辐射流体动力学模拟提出，年轻大质量恒星联合体产生的H II区可贡献 \( {\rm DM}_{\rm HII} \gtrsim 10^3\;{\rm pc\;cm^{-3}} \)，从而解释重复FRB的局域DM超出及其对低金属丰度矮星系宿主的偏好，其中尘埃吸收在较高金属丰度下抑制电离柱密度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多个重复FRB源表现出远超宿主星系星际介质预期的宿主坐标系DM超出，常被归因于超新星遗迹，但该经典解释无法说明其对低金属丰度矮星系宿主的偏好。本研究旨在检验年轻大质量恒星联合体产生的H II区能否同时解释这些DM超出及其与低金属丰度矮星系的关联。

Method: 作者为在成团分子云中发射FRB的中子星建立半解析模型，并使用来自pySTARBURST99的时间依赖电离光度进行理想化的一维辐射流体动力学模拟，考察不同质量、年龄和金属丰度条件下的电离柱密度贡献。

Result: 结果表明，在低金属丰度环境（类似LMC或更低）中，大质量（\(M_\star \sim 10^5-10^6\;M_\odot\)）、年轻（\(\lesssim 10\;{\rm Myr}\)）的恒星联合体可产生 \({\rm DM}_{\rm HII} \gtrsim 10^3\;{\rm pc\;cm^{-3}}\)，前提是FRB形成于早期（\(\lesssim 5\) Myr）并保持在电离气体中。低质量/低金属丰度偏好源于尘埃对莱曼连续谱的吸收，在较高金属丰度下抑制电离柱密度。

Conclusion: 作者提出极端局域DM优先在贫尘、低金属丰度环境中产生，从而连接了重复FRB的DM超出与其低金属丰度矮星系宿主偏好。据此建议，在宇宙学距离上选取金属丰度高、质量大且宁静的宿主星系样本（此类极端局域贡献可能受到抑制），以获得更干净的FRB样本，用于以星系际DM示踪中介星系际介质和星系周介质。

Abstract: Several repeating fast radio burst (FRB) sources exhibit host-frame dispersion measure (DM) excesses far exceeding those expected from the interstellar medium in their host galaxies, implying a substantial contribution from the sub-kiloparsec-scale local environment. These excesses are often attributed to an expanding supernova remnant; however, this canonical interpretation does not explain their apparent preference for low-metallicity dwarf galaxy hosts, suggesting that the mechanism producing the local DM is coupled to the properties of the host galaxy. In this work, we investigate whether the H II regions generated by young, massive stellar associations can simultaneously explain these DM excesses and their preferential association with low-metallicity dwarf galaxies. We develop a semi-analytic model for FRB-emitting neutron stars in cluster-forming molecular clouds and perform idealized one-dimensional radiation-hydrodynamic simulations using time-dependent ionizing luminosities from pySTARBURST99. Our findings indicate that high-mass $(M_\star \sim 10^5-10^6\;M_\odot)$, young ($\lesssim 10\;{\rm Myr}$) stellar associations in low-metallicity environments (LMC-like or lower) can produce ${\rm DM}_{\rm HII} \gtrsim 10^3\;{\rm pc\;cm^{-3}}$ for FRBs that form early ($\lesssim 5$ Myr) and remain embedded in the ionized gas. The low-mass/metallicity preference arises from dust: absorption of the Lyman continuum suppresses the ionized column at higher metallicity, making extreme local DMs preferentially generated in dust-poor, low-metallicity environments. At cosmological distances, selection of a metal-rich, massive, and quiescent host galaxy sample where such extreme local contributions are likely to be suppressed may yield a cleaner FRB sample for using extragalactic DMs as tracers of the intervening intergalactic and circumgalactic media.

### [4] [JUG: JAX-based Unified pulsar timinG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27786)
*Miles, Matthew T.,Taylor, Stephen R.,Bailes, Matthew,Chalumeau, Aurelien,Cromartie, H. Thankful,Gersbach, Kyle A.,van Haasteren, Rutger,Keith, Michael J.,Laal, Nima,Lam, Michael T.,Liu, Kuo,Parthasarathy, Aditya,Ransom, Scott M.,Reardon, Daniel J.,Shannon, Ryan M.,Wright, David C.,Zic, Andrew*

Main category: scix

TL;DR: JUG是基于JAX的独立脉冲星计时软件包，比PINT快五十倍以上、与Tempo2相当，可处理数百万到达时间，与PINT在皮秒级一致，并能可靠恢复已知的计时模型与噪声参数值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 脉冲星计时阵列领域正产生规模越来越大、越来越复杂的数据集，现有计时软件在速度和易用性方面面临挑战，本文旨在开发一个完全独立且快速、易于使用的脉冲星计时软件包，以应对这些大规模数据。

Method: 摘要表明采用JAX框架实现整个脉冲星计时流程，包括数据处理、时钟修正、计时模型与拟合，不依赖其他计时软件；支持GPU，可通过Python API或交互式GUI操作；文中描述了JUG的设计、性能与验证，并将其速度与PINT和Tempo2作比较。

Result: JUG比PINT快五十多倍，与Tempo2速度相当，可处理数百万到达时间，与PINT在皮秒级别一致，并能可靠恢复已知的计时模型与噪声参数值；用户可交互探索数据、拟合带复杂随机噪声的计时模型、建模连续引力波等确定性信号。

Conclusion: 作者认为JUG展示了其对脉冲星计时数据分析的优势，能够在频率派计时与贝叶斯噪声分析之间架起桥梁。基于摘要无法判断其脉冲星、磁星或FRB现象学方面的物理结论；该工作的贡献属方法学层面。

Abstract: We present JUG (JAX-based Unified pulsar timinG), a JAX-based, fully independent pulsar timing package emphasising speed and ease of use, designed to confidently handle the increasingly large and complex pulsar timing array datasets that are being created in the pulsar timing field. JUG implements the entire pulsar timing pipeline itself, from data handling and clock corrections through to the timing model and fitting, without relying on other timing software. It enables Pythonic programming at the speed of compiled code, is GPU-capable, and can be operated via a Python API or an interactive GUI. A user of JUG can interactively explore data, fit timing models with complex stochastic noise, model deterministic signals such as continuous gravitational waves, and obtain accurate point estimates of the parameters of the stochastic processes present, thereby bridging frequentist timing and Bayesian noise analysis. JUG is faster than PINT by more than fifty times and is comparably fast to Tempo2, can handle millions of arrival times, agrees with PINT at the picosecond level, and can reliably recover known timing model and noise parameter values. In this paper we describe its design, performance, and validation, and demonstrate its advantages for pulsar timing data analysis.
