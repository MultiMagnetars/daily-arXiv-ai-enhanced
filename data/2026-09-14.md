### [1] [Spin-polarization of the neutron ocean in magnetar crusts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12539)
*Servais, Juliette,Chamel, Nicolas*

Main category: scix

TL;DR: 在中子自旋极化作用下，$B \gtrsim 10^{17}~\mathrm{G}$ 的强磁场会同时降低磁星壳层内外壳层边界处的压强与物质密度，使外壳层变薄、先前预言的超重核形成受抑制，并令内壳层中的中子海洋变为自旋极化；这些效应可能影响磁星的各种表现与磁星巨耀发射出物中的 $r$-过程核合成。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 磁星与从巨耀发到快速射电暴的广泛观测现象相关，并被认为是重元素核合成的潜在场所；这些现象被认为与磁星壳层的结构和组成密切相关。壳层分为由完全电离原子核与简并电子气组成的外壳层，以及额外含自由中子的内壳层。已有研究预言超重核的形成，但强磁场如何通过自由中子自旋极化改壳层结构尚待考察。

Method: 摘要描述了在强磁场下研究磁星壳层的理论工作，明确考虑自由中子的自旋极化，该极化源于磁场与中子磁矩之间的耦合。摘要未说明所用的具体模型、状态方程、数值方法、样本或观测数据。

Result: 对于 $B \gtrsim 10^{17}~\mathrm{G}$，中子自旋极化同时降低了标志外壳层与内壳层边界的压强和物质密度，导致外壳层变得更薄，先前研究中预言的超重核形成受到抑制，并且渗透内壳层的中子海洋变为自旋极化。摘要未说明具体的数值结果或显著性。

Conclusion: 作者认为这些效应可能对磁星的多种表现形式以及磁星巨耀发射出物中的 $r$-过程核合成具有重要意义。摘要未给出该结论的观测检验；与脉冲星、磁星、快速射电暴等具体观测现象的直接联系，根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: Magnetars, neutron stars harboring the strongest magnetic fields known in the Universe, are associated with a broad range of observational phenomena, from giant flares to fast radio bursts, and have also been proposed as potential sites of heavy-element nucleosynthesis. These phenomena are thought to be closely linked to the structure and composition of magnetar crusts. The outer crust consists of fully ionized nuclei embedded in a degenerate electron gas, while the inner crust additionally contains free neutrons. We investigate how strong magnetic fields modify magnetar crusts, accounting for the spin polarization of free neutrons. Arising from the coupling between the magnetic field and the neutron magnetic moment, neutron spin polarization lowers both the pressure and matter density marking the boundary between the outer and inner crusts for magnetic field strengths $B \gtrsim 10^{17}~\mathrm{G}$. As a consequence, the outer crust becomes thinner, the formation of the superheavy nuclei predicted in previous studies is suppressed, and the neutron ocean permeating the inner crust becomes spin-polarized. These effects may have important implications for the diverse manifestations of magnetars and for $r$-process nucleosynthesis in the ejecta of magnetar giant flares.

### [2] [Optical Follow-Up of Two Spider Pulsar Candidates: An Irradiated Redback and a Possible Double Giant Impostor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12618)
*Due, Bettina,Bridziute, Jundile,Linares, Manuel,Turchetta, Marco,Satybaldiev, Maksat*

Main category: scix

TL;DR: 对两个蜘蛛脉冲星候选体的光学后续观测表明，J0846并非真正的脉冲星候选体，而被重新认证为一个分离的双红巨星；J0935则显示出伴星上合附近由脉冲星辐照引起的流量与温度升高，支持其为处于脉冲星状态的红背蜘蛛。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 银河系场中已知超过100个蜘蛛脉冲星系统，其中超过30个候选体尚未探测到脉冲信号，因此需要多波段观测来判定这些候选体的真实本质与当前状态。

Method: 对两个蜘蛛脉冲星候选体J0846和J0935进行光学后续观测，并分析其红外、X射线和$γ$射线波段的多波段性质；利用光学轨道光变曲线、光谱能量分布以及颜色和推断温度的变化来评估其本质。摘要未说明所用望远镜或仪器、观测频段、时间跨度与样本量的具体细节。

Result: J0846的光学轨道光变曲线从2023年的双峰变为2024年的单峰，但不与辐照驱动或椭球轨道调制一致；其光谱能量分布显示两个温度与半径相近的黑体状成分，分别主导光学波段（$T_2\simeq6000$ K，$R_2\simeq1.8$ R$_\odot$）和红外波段（$T_1\simeq3400$ K，$R_1\simeq5.2$ R$_\odot$）。J0935的光变曲线在伴星上合附近有一个流量极大值，此处颜色和推断温度升高，伴星基线温度被限制在$4000\!-\!4600$ K。

Conclusion: 作者认为J0846不是真正的脉冲星候选体，并提出其为分离的双红巨星；J0935的光变与温度特征表明存在来自脉冲星的显著辐照，其伴星基线温度限制支持此前提出的三种分类之一，即处于脉冲星状态的红背蜘蛛。

Abstract: Compact binary millisecond pulsars (nicknamed "spiders") are a growing class of neutron stars: more than 100 systems are known in the Galactic field, including more than 30 candidates where pulsations have not been detected. Here we report the results of an optical follow-up of two spider pulsar candidates: 2FGL J0846.0+2820 (J0846, a huntsman candidate in an 8.1 d orbit) and 4FGL J0935.3+0901 (J0935, with a 2.4 h orbital period and proposed as a spider of either the transitional, black widow or redback sub-class). We also analyzed their multi-wavelength properties (infrared, X-ray and $γ$-ray bands), in order to assess their nature and current state. The optical orbital light curves of J0846 change from double-peaked in 2023 to single-peaked in 2024, but we argue they are not consistent with either irradiation-driven or ellipsoidal orbital modulation. Its spectral energy distribution shows evidence for two blackbody-like components with similar temperatures ($T$) and radii ($R$), which dominate the optical ($T_2\simeq6000$ K, $R_2\simeq1.8$ R$_\odot$) and infrared ($T_1\simeq3400$ K, $R_1\simeq5.2$ R$_\odot$) bands. Based on this, we argue that J0846 is not a bona fide pulsar candidate and propose an alternative identification: a detached double red giant. In J0935, we find a light curve with one flux maximum around superior conjunction of the companion, where the colors and inferred temperature increase, suggesting prominent irradiation from the pulsar. We place constraints on its companion base temperature ($4000\!-\!4600$ K), which support one of the three classifications proposed for J0935: a redback spider in the pulsar state.

### [3] [The Role of the Core in Setting Massive Neutron-Star Radii](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11967)
*Lobato, R. V.,Horvath, J. E.,Malheiro, M.*

Main category: scix

TL;DR: 该论文表明，对于大质量脉冲星（如 PSR J0740+6620，$2.08\,M_{\odot}$），其半径的主要径向尺度可由约两倍核饱和密度处起始的相对论性高声速核心设定，而低密度外层只起次主导修正；这种核心主导随质量增大而系统性出现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: NICER 观测表明大质量脉冲星 PSR J0740+6620（$2.08\,M_{\odot}$）的半径与接近 $1.4\,M_{\odot}$ 的 PSR J0030+0451 及更新分析所推断的半径相当；除非高密度状态方程强烈变硬，这种近乎垂直的质量-半径行为难以获得，因此需要理解大质量脉冲星半径由哪一部分设定。

Method: 结合解析 Tolman VII 剖面与薄壳匹配、使用分段 EOS 的数值 Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff 积分，以及在因果性和质量支撑筛选下使用 NICER 和 GW170817 约束的直接网格贝叶斯推断；通过将匹配点以下的低密度分支替换为在转变压强上跨越两倍的统一 EOS 模型进行半径分解，并进行固定比例比较。

Result: 将匹配点以下的低密度分支替换为在转变压强上跨越两倍的统一 EOS 模型，仅使 $2.08\,M_{\odot}$ 的核心半径改变约 $\sim160$ m，约为总半径变化的一半；在 $1.4\,M_{\odot}$ 处核心半径对分支仍更敏感，变化约 $0.5$ km。固定比例比较确认核心主导随恒星质量增大而系统性出现。数据对转变刚度给出适度约束，但使二次变硬主要由因果性决定；与恒定声速核心的比较仍未得出定论，两种描述产生可比较的核心主导半径。

Conclusion: 作者认为，对于大质量脉冲星，主导径向尺度可由约两倍核饱和密度处起始的相对论性高声速核心设定，外部低密度层仅提供次主导修正；核心主导随质量增大而系统性出现，此时“半径物理”与“最大质量物理”之间的传统区分失效。与恒定声速核心的简并可由亚公里级半径测量打破。根据摘要无法判断该图像对脉冲星、磁星或快速射电暴相关现象的具体适用性。

Abstract: Recent observations by the Neutron Star Interior Composition Explorer (NICER) indicate that the massive pulsar PSR J0740+6620 ($2.08\,M_{\odot}$) has a radius comparable to those inferred for stars near $1.4\,M_{\odot}$, as shown by PSR J0030+0451 and updated analyses. Such near-vertical mass-radius behavior is difficult to obtain unless the high-density equation of state (EOS) stiffens strongly. We show that, for massive pulsars, the leading radial scale can be set by a relativistic, high-sound-velocity core beginning near twice the nuclear saturation scale, while the outer low-density layer, comprising the true crust and outer core, supplies only a subdominant correction. The key evidence comes from radius decomposition: replacing the low-density branch below the matching point with unified EOS models spanning a factor of two in transition pressure changes the $2.08\,M_{\odot}$ core radius by only $\sim160$ m, about half the variation of the total radius. This convergence is genuinely a high-mass phenomenon: at $1.4\,M_{\odot}$ the core radius remains more branch-sensitive, varying by about $0.5$ km. Fixed-fraction comparisons confirm that core dominance emerges systematically as the star grows more massive, where the traditional separation between ``radius physics'' and ``maximum-mass physics'' breaks down. We establish this picture by combining analytical Tolman VII profiles and thin-crust matching, numerical Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff integration with a piecewise EOS, and direct-grid Bayesian inference using NICER and GW170817 constraints under causal and mass-support filters. Data modestly constrain the transition stiffness but leave the quadratic stiffening governed mainly by causality. A comparison with a constant-sound-speed core remains inconclusive: both descriptions produce comparable core-dominated radii, leaving a degeneracy that sub-kilometre radius measurements can break.

### [4] [BAQARO: Tracing Stochastic Black Hole Growth Histories and Quasar Lightcurves in a Cosmological Context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12061)
*Pizzati, Elia,Hennawi, Joseph F.,Schaye, Joop*

Main category: scix

TL;DR: BAQARO 是一个基于 FLAMINGO-10k 子晕并合树的半经验黑洞增长框架，能再现 $0 \lesssim z \lesssim 7$ 的类星体观测约束，并表明高红移黑洞通过持续约 $\sim1\,\mathrm{Myr}$ 的随机超爱丁顿吸积事件快速组装质量，而并合增长在 $z \lesssim 1$ 时变得更重要。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超大质量黑洞如何组装质量并驱动宇宙历史中观测到的明亮类星体，仍是星系演化中的核心开放问题。论文旨在构建一个能够连接黑洞增长历史与宿主子晕增长历史、并同时刻画平均趋势和随机可变性的框架。

Method: 摘要说明 BAQARO 建立在从 FLAMINGO-10k 模拟中提取的子晕并合树上，通过 prescriptions 将黑洞增长历史与宿主子晕增长历史相联系，并由热辐射类星体光度函数、紫外明亮类星体成团性和条件爱丁顿比分布函数约束。模型有六个自由参数，分别控制黑洞种子、气体吸积与晕增长的耦合以及吸积的随机性和时间相干性；作者使用 emulators 进行贝叶斯推断并量化各观测量约束能力。

Result: 模型在 $0 \lesssim z \lesssim 7$ 范围内重现现有观测约束。主要发现包括：黑洞吸积可由晕中冷气体储库的组装很好地描述，无需显式依赖宇宙时间；黑洞在高红移通过随机超爱丁顿吸积事件快速组装质量；这些事件辐射效率低下并持续约 $\sim1\,\mathrm{Myr}$，在类星体光变曲线、邻近区和成团性测量中留下可观测印记；并合增长通道始终次要，但在 $z \lesssim 1$ 时越来越重要，尤其对质量较大的黑洞。

Conclusion: 作者认为 BAQARO 的增长历史和类星体光变曲线为解释快速扩展的类星体观测图景提供了灵活框架，可涵盖由 JWST 探测的高 $z$ 吸积到由 pulsar timing arrays 约束的低 $z$ 并合。对于该框架是否可直接联系脉冲星、磁星、单脉冲发射、模式转换、射电偏振或 FRB，摘要未说明；根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: How supermassive black holes (BHs) assemble their mass and power the luminous quasars we observe across cosmic time remain central open questions in galaxy evolution. We present BAQARO, a semi-empirical framework for BH growth. Built on subhalo merger trees extracted from the FLAMINGO-10k simulation, the model links BH growth histories to those of their host subhalos through prescriptions that capture both average trends and stochastic variability. BAQARO is constrained by the bolometric quasar luminosity function, the clustering of UV-luminous quasars, and the conditional Eddington ratio distribution function. With six free parameters controlling BH seeding, the coupling between gas accretion and halo growth, and the stochasticity and temporal coherence of accretion, the model reproduces the available observational constraints over $0 \lesssim z \lesssim 7$. Using emulators, we perform Bayesian inference and quantify the constraining power of each observable. Our main findings are: (i) BH accretion is well described by the assembly of cold gas reservoirs in halos, without requiring an explicit dependence on cosmic time. (ii) BHs rapidly assemble their mass at high redshift through stochastic episodes of super-Eddington accretion. (iii) These episodes are radiatively inefficient and persist for timescales of $\sim1\,\mathrm{Myr}$, imprinting observable signatures on quasar lightcurves, proximity zones, and clustering measurements. (iv) The merger growth channel is always subdominant, but becomes increasingly important at $z \lesssim 1$, particularly for massive BHs. BAQARO growth histories and quasar lightcurves provide a flexible framework for interpreting the rapidly expanding landscape of quasar observations, from high-$z$ accretion probed by JWST to low-$z$ mergers constrained by pulsar timing arrays.
