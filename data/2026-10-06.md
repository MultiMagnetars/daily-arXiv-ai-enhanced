Although the currently known ULXPs are unfavorable CGW targets because of their relatively slow spins and, in most cases, large distances, our illustrative spin-evolution calculations show that a NS can enter the super-Eddington accretor phase with a spin period of order $20\,{\rm ms}$. Such rapidly rotating Galactic ULXPs could lie within the projected sensitivities of next-generation detectors such as the Einstein Telescope and Cosmic Explorer. These results extend thermal-mountain physics into the strongly magnetized regime relevant to ULXPs and connect super-Eddington accretion with prospective CGW observations.
