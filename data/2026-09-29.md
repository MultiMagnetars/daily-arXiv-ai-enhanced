Result: 注入-恢复模拟显示，标准的计数论证会导致对数据的严重过拟合；所提出的数据驱动方法将模型参数数量减少了一到三个数量级，并且在数据支持时可达到比标准计数论证更高的分辨率。

Conclusion: 作者得出结论：在脉冲星计时阵 GWB 各向异性分析中，应以数据驱动方法替代朴素计数论证来设定地图分辨率，从而避免严重过拟合，并在减少参数数量的同时仍可获得高分辨率。根据摘要无法判断该方法与脉冲星、磁星、单脉冲、模式转换、偏振、射电谱或 FRB 等具体物理问题的联系。

Abstract: Pulsar timing array anisotropy analyses often use a naive counting argument to set resolutions of inferred maps of the stochastic gravitational wave background (GWB). We present a data-driven method in the form of a delayed rejection reversible jump sampler tailored to multi-resolution pixel decompositions of the angular power density of the GWB. We also consider a rapid, frequentist alternative based on information criteria statistics. We verify our methods with a series of injection-and-recovery simulations, finding that the standard counting argument would lead to drastic overfitting of the data and that our data-driven methods reduce the number of parameters in the models by one to three orders of magnitude yet can achieve higher resolution than the standard counting argument when justified by the data. We make our sampler and frequentist method implementation available on GitHub.
