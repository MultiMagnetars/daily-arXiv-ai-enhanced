### [1] [A mechanism for fast radio bursts and their narrow spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21076)
*Andrei M. Beloborodov,Ivan Demidov*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该文提出一种在磁星爆炸中运行的 FRB 辐射机制：脉冲内等离子体向外加速，使环境 kHz 波经受激散射并以 $Γ^2$ 增强转换为径向 GHz 辐射，从而将微小 GHz 种子指数式放大至与观测 FRB 相符的光度，并预测饱和区制下存在超强微秒分量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 论文指出 FRB 既可能具有宽谱也可能具有窄谱，并提出一种能够产生宽谱和窄谱的辐射机制，以解释 FRB 的辐射与谱形。摘要未说明更具体的既有理论困难或观测矛盾。

Method: 摘要描述的是理论机制分析：考虑发生在磁星爆炸中的过程，爆炸从内磁层半径 $r_0\sim 10^{7}$ cm 处发射电磁脉冲，脉冲内等离子体以 $Γ(r)\approx r/r_0$ 向外加速；脉冲在半径 $r\sim 10^{10}$ cm 处离开磁层前穿过环境 kHz 波，并分析这些波在脉冲内部的受激散射及其向径向 GHz 辐射的转换。摘要未说明所用望远镜、观测频段、时间跨度、样本或统计方法。

Result: 摘要报告，脉冲内部等离子体以 $Γ(r)\approx r/r_0$ 向外加速；脉冲在离开磁层前穿过充满活跃磁星磁层的环境 kHz 波。这些波在脉冲内部经历受激散射，$Γ^2$ 增强因子将其转换为径向 GHz 辐射；该过程将微小的 GHz 种子指数式放大至极高光度，与观测到的 FRB 相符。产生的 GHz 爆发乘坐在爆炸脉冲内部，有助于逃逸至遥远观测者；FRB 的持续时间和能量由脉冲的持续时间和能量控制；在饱和辐射区制下，预测爆发包含一个超强微秒分量。

Conclusion: 作者提出的物理图像是：磁星爆炸从内磁层发射电磁脉冲，环境 kHz 波在脉冲内受激散射并转换为径向 GHz 辐射，使微小 GHz 种子指数式放大到与观测 FRB 相符的极高光度；GHz 爆发乘坐在爆炸脉冲内部而得以逃逸，且其持续时间和能量由脉冲控制，饱和区制下还预测存在超强微秒分量。根据摘要无法判断该机制与脉冲星单脉冲、脉冲轮廓演化、模式转换、计时或偏振观测之间的具体联系。

Abstract: We find an emission mechanism for fast radio bursts (FRBs) capable of producing broad and narrow spectra. It operates in magnetar explosions, which launch an electromagnetic pulse from the inner magnetosphere at radii $r_0\sim 10^{7}$ cm. Plasma inside the pulse accelerates outward with Lorentz factor $Γ(r)\approx r/r_0$. Before exiting the magnetosphere at radius $r\sim 10^{10}$ cm, the pulse passes through ambient kHz waves that populate the magnetospheres of active magnetars. We show that the waves experience stimulated scattering inside the pulse, with the boost factor $Γ^2$ converting them into radial GHz radiation. This process exponentially amplifies tiny GHz seeds to extreme luminosities consistent with observed FRBs. The produced GHz burst rides inside the explosion pulse, which helps it escape to distant observers. The FRB duration and energy are controlled by the pulse duration and energy. In a saturated emission regime, the predicted burst contains an ultrastrong microsecond component.

### [2] [CRAFT HTR2: Polarimetry of 64 non-repeating fast radio bursts from the updated CRAFT catalogue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21177)
*T. Dial,A. T. Deller,Marcin Glowacki,A. Bera,R. M. Shannon,Clancy W. James,Joscha N. Jahns-Schindler,Ziteng Wang,Akhil Jaini*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: CRAFT 巡天新增34个快速射电暴的高时间分辨率谱偏振数据使 CRAFT HTR 星表规模翻倍，并与 CHIME、DSA 星表比较了河外 RM 与偏振度分布：CRAFT 与 DSA 无显著差异，而 CHIME 的河外 RM 分布显著更低；三个星表的线偏振度分布无显著差异。作者给出较宽、较暗弱暴偏振度较低的初步证据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: CRAFT HTR 星表需要扩充以纳入更高灵敏度探测系统带来的新样本，并检验不同望远镜观测频率下 FRB 的河外 RM 分布与偏振度分布是否存在差异，以及较宽、较暗弱暴与未偏振暴之间是否存在关联。

Method: 基于 Commensal Real-time Fast Transients（CRAFT）巡天利用 ASKAP 在2024年5月至2026年6月期间发现的34个新 FRB 的高时间分辨率谱偏振数据；其中大多数由期间调试完成的 CRACO 高灵敏度探测系统探测到。将河外 RM 和偏振度分布与 CHIME 和 DSA 星表进行比较，并分析偏振度与暴宽度、亮度之间的关系。

Result: 新样本使 CRAFT HTR 星表规模翻倍，并探测到更暗弱的 FRB 族群。CRAFT 与 DSA 的河外 RM 分布无显著差异；在较低频率探测的 CHIME FRB 中，河外 RM 分布显著更低。三个 FRB 星表之间的线偏振度分布无显著差异。作者发现较宽且较暗弱的暴具有较低偏振度的初步证据，这主要由不断增长的未偏振暴样本驱动；这些未偏振暴在几乎所有情况下都比 ASKAP 探测中位数更宽（$\gg$10 ms）且更暗弱（$\ll$10$^{34}$ ergs s$^{-1}$ Hz$^{-1}$）。

Conclusion: 作者认为，CRAFT、CHIME 与 DSA 之间线偏振度分布缺乏显著差异表明其对不同望远镜观测频率并不敏感；而 CHIME 中显著更低的河外 RM 分布与 CRAFT、DSA 之间的无显著差异形成对比。较宽、较暗弱暴偏振度较低的证据仍属初步，且主要由未偏振暴样本驱动。CRAFT 与 DSA 无显著差异、CHIME 河外 RM 分布较低等现象背后的具体物理机制，根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: We present high-time resolution spectro-polarimetric data for 34 new fast radio bursts (FRBs) discovered by the Commensal Real-time Fast Transients (CRAFT) survey on the Australian Square Kilometer Array Pathfinder (ASKAP) during the period May 2024 to June 2026. Most of these were detected by the higher-sensitivity CRAFT COherent (CRACO) detection system that was commissioned on the telescope during this period. This new sample doubles the size of the CRAFT HTR catalogue and probes a fainter population of FRBs thanks to the improved sensitivity of CRACO. We compare the distribution of extragalactic rotation measure (RM) and polarisation fraction to the CHIME and DSA catalogues. While no significant differences were seen between CRAFT and DSA, the extragalactic RM distribution seen in CHIME FRBs (which are detected at lower frequency) was substantially lower. Surprisingly, we find no significant differences in the linear polarisation fraction distribution between the three FRB catalogues, suggesting an indifference to the different telescope observing frequencies. We show tentative evidence for wider and fainter bursts possessing lower polarisation fractions; this is predominantly driven by the growing sample of unpolarised bursts that are, in almost all cases, wider ($\gg$10 ms) and fainter ($\ll$10$^{34}$ ergs s$^{-1}$ Hz$^{-1}$) than the median ASKAP detection.

</details>
