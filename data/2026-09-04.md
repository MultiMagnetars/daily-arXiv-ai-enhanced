### [1] [Single-pulse reanalysis of the 2024 Vela glitch and new observations of PSR~J0437$-$4715 and PSR~J1644$-$4559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03056)
*Carlos O. Lousto,Ruby Shrestha,Ezequiel Zubieta,Susana B. Araujo Furlan,Guillermo Gancio,Federico García,Santiago del Palacio,Ryan Missel,Linwei Wang*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该论文对Vela 2024年大自转突变进行了基于机器学习的逐脉冲重新分析，以七倍带宽重现了高速簇更早、更窄、更偏斜且更少的行为，并验证了恢复项缺失会造成轮廓展宽的计时假象；同时将流水线扩展到PSR~J1644$-$4559与毫秒脉冲星PSR~J0437$-$4715，后者聚类可取得约2倍信噪比提升并潜在改善计时精度。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: PuMA合作项目旨在系统监测南天发生自转突变的脉冲星并维持高节奏单脉冲记录，检验机器学习的单脉冲聚类流水线能否扩展到其他源，以及验证自转突变期间的轮廓稳定性与聚类行为对宽带数据、嵌套恢复项计时的依赖。

Method: 利用阿根廷射电天文研究所400~MHz带宽ROACH后端对Vela进行逐脉冲重新分析，并将机器学习单脉冲流水线（Isolation Forest离群剔除、$β$-Variational-AutoEncoder去噪、Self-Organizing-Map聚类）扩展到PSR~J1644$-$4559和PSR~J0437$-$4715的新观测。对Vela自转突变前后八天数据做4簇与6簇分解，并与先前窄带ETTUS接收机结果比较，计时解含跳变及两个指数恢复项。

Result: 对Vela，4簇与6簇分解以七倍带宽重现窄带结果：高振幅簇在相位上更早达峰、更窄、更偏斜且更少。含跳变与两个指数恢复项的计时解使平均轮廓八天内稳定在1\%以内（宽度变化$-0.5\pm0.9$\%)；省略恢复项会以$10^{-7}$级折叠频率误差模拟高达55\%的展宽。PSR~J1644$-$4559簇几乎仅在振幅上不同。对PSR~J0437$-$4715，保留峰值主导得分前10\%的脉冲使信噪比加倍，五簇分解组比平均轮廓窄$3.6\pm0.4$倍，提示约3.5倍计时精度提升。

Conclusion: 作者将Vela宽带数据的行为解释为证实并扩展了先前窄带结果，表明轮廓对自转突变的稳定性高度依赖正确的计时模型（含指数恢复项），否则会被误解为突变后展宽。对PSR~J0437$-$4715，作者提出基于聚类的计时精度可能约有3.5倍的改进，并明确表示为待后续论文确认的推测性结论。根据摘要无法判断这些结果与磁星或FRB之间的物理联系。

Abstract: The Pulsar Monitoring in Argentina (PuMA) collaboration systematically monitors southern glitching pulsars, maintaining high-cadence single-pulse records of the Vela pulsar. We present a pulse-per-pulse reanalysis of the 2024 major glitch of Vela (PSR~J0835$-$4510) with the 400~MHz-bandwidth ROACH backend of the Argentine Institute of Radioastronomy, and extend our machine-learning single-pulse pipeline---Isolation Forest outlier rejection, $β$-Variational-AutoEncoder denoising, and Self-Organizing-Map clustering---to new observations of PSR~J1644$-$4559 and the millisecond pulsar PSR~J0437$-$4715. For Vela, the 4- and 6-cluster decompositions of the eight days bracketing the glitch reproduce, with seven times the previous bandwidth, the behavior found with the narrow-band ETTUS receivers: higher-amplitude clusters peak earlier in phase, are narrower, more skewed, and less populated. With the glitch jump and its two exponential recovery terms included in the timing solution, the mean profile is stable to 1\% across all eight days (width change $-0.5\pm0.9$\% from pre- to post-glitch); omitting the recovery terms would mimic a post-glitch broadening of up to 55\% through a folding-frequency error at the $10^{-7}$ level. The clusters of PSR~J1644$-$4559 differ almost exclusively in amplitude, as expected for a scattering-dominated profile. For PSR~J0437$-$4715, retaining the 10\% of pulses with the highest peak-dominance score doubles the signal-to-noise ratio, and a five-cluster decomposition yields narrow, phase-ordered groups a factor $3.6\pm0.4$ narrower than the average profile, suggesting a $\sim$3.5-fold improvement in cluster-based timing precision for this pulsar-timing-array target, to be confirmed in a follow-up paper.

### [2] [Thermal and non-thermal emission from supermassive black hole circumbinary disks: Disks, Coronae, Streams, and Cavities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03140)
*Chris Nagele,Julian H. Krolik,Brooks E. Kinch,Jeremy D. Schnittman,Scott Noble*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 通过对间距20$ M$、0.01爱丁顿吸积的双超大质量黑洞系统的HARM3D模拟快照进行辐射后处理，作者自洽求解了不同黑洞质量的辐射光谱，发现约40%的观测光度来自软X射线幂律谱，且存在两种具有相同周期、相位相差约π/2的方位角变化模式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探测SMBH双星系统的电磁信号是多信使天体物理学的核心目标之一，可补充脉冲星计时阵列和LISA的引力波观测。尽管已有大量模拟研究其热光度的时变，但光谱预测很少且未超出简单发射模型，本文旨在填补这一空白。

Method: 后处理一个间距为20$ M$、以0.01爱丁顿吸积的双星系统的\texttt{HARM3D}模拟快照；针对黑洞质量$10^6$、$10^7$和$10^8\, M_\odot$，基于时间稳态辐射转移、热能平衡与电离平衡自洽求解辐射光谱，并包含相对论效应及发射、吸收过程。摘要未说明具体观测频率或时间跨度。

Result: 大部分热光度由盘热辐射发出，但双星四极矩排空的低密度区域产生丰富X射线，约40%的观测光度来自软X射线幂律谱（Γ=2.3）。识别出两种方位角变化模式：X射线连续谱因螺旋激波气体温度不对称性变化约10%；Fe Kα等效宽度因团块部分遮挡内盘视线而下降约25%。两者周期相同、相位差约π/2，周期量级为几天到几周。

Conclusion: 作者提出低密度区域产生的软X射线幂律谱以及螺旋激波温度和遮挡效应驱动的X射线与Fe Kα方位角变化，可作为SMBH双星系统的电磁特征。摘要未说明将这些模型特征直接外推至脉冲星、磁星或FRB的具体机制。

Abstract: The search for electromagnetic signals from supermassive black hole (SMBH) binary systems is one of the cornerstones of multi-messenger astrophysics, complementing gravitational wave observations of such systems by pulsar timing arrays and LISA. Although extensive simulations have been run to understand the time variability of the bolometric luminosity from accreting binary SMBH systems, comparatively few spectral predictions have been made, and none that go beyond simple emission models. In this paper, we post-process a \texttt{HARM3D} simulation snapshot of a binary at $20 M$ separation accreting at 0.01 Eddington. For black hole masses $10^6$, $10^7$, and $10^8\, M_\odot$, we self-consistently solve for the radiated spectrum on the basis of time-steady radiation transfer, thermal balance, and ionization equilibrium, including all relevant relativistic effects as well as emission and absorption processes. Although most of the bolometric luminosity is radiated thermally by the disk, the low density regions evacuated by the binary's quadrupole moment produce copious X-rays, with $\sim40\%$ of the observed luminosity in a soft X-ray power law ($Γ= 2.3$). We identify two modes of observed azimuthal variation. The X-ray continuum varies by $\sim10\%$ due to an underlying asymmetry in the gas temperature of spiral shocks in the disk. The Fe~K$α$ equivalent width dips by $\sim25\%$ when the line of sight to the inner disk is partially obscured by the lump. These two effects share the same period and are $\sim π/2$ out of phase; the period is order days to weeks for typical AGN masses and a 20$M$ separation.

### [3] [Observational selection effects on radio pulsars are minimal for masses, but significant for orbits and spins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03157)
*Lisa V. Drummond,Katerina Chatziioannou,Emmanuel Fonseca*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 这篇论文通过模拟射电计时观测并将选择函数纳入分层族群推断，发现选择效应对脉冲星质量分布影响极小，但对轨道偏心率和周期、自转周期影响显著，真实族群（圆偏比2:1，更长周期）不同于观测结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 银河系双星脉冲星的质量分布呈双峰且在大约$2\,M_\odot$处截断，对致密物质与天体形成有启示，但观测种群同时受内在性质与射电计时相关的观测选择效应影响（可探测性与可测量性），本文旨在厘清选择效应以获得内在天体物理分布。

Method: 模拟射电脉冲星系统与计时观测，追踪系统进入观测数据集的完整过程，估计双星与脉冲星参数的影响；将选择函数纳入分层族群推断（hierarchical population inference），对脉冲星质量、伴星质量、轨道偏心率、轨道周期与自转周期族群进行联合推断。摘要未说明具体望远镜、频率、时间跨度或样本数量。

Result: 选择效应对质量分布的影响极小，但对其他参数影响更显著：圆轨道与偏心系统比例从观测的$1\!:\!1$变为经选择校正后的天体物理$2\!:\!1$；真实族群比观测值更偏向更长的轨道周期和自转周期。

Conclusion: 作者基于模拟与族群推断得出结论：射电脉冲星的射电质量分布可视为接近内在分布（选择效应影响很小），而轨道与自转参数的观测分布被选择效应明显扭曲，需经校正才能反映真实天体物理种群。根据摘要无法判断本文结果对磁星或FRB的转移意义。

Abstract: The masses of Galactic pulsars in binaries measured through radio timing point to structure in the mass distribution with implications for dense matter and astrophysical formation processes: a bimodal shape with peaks at ${\sim} 1.3\,M_\odot$ and ${\sim} 1.6\,M_\odot$, with a cutoff above ${\sim}2\,M_\odot$. However, this observed population of radio pulsars is shaped not only by intrinsic pulsar properties, such as birth masses, binary evolutionary pathways and dense-matter constraints, but also by observational effects. In existing catalogs of pulsar mass measurements, observational selection effects influence (i) which systems are detected in the first place (detectability) and (ii) which systems yield well-measured masses (measurability). In this work, we revisit the radio pulsar population distribution in the context of observational effects associated with pulsar radio timing. Simulating systems of radio pulsars and timing observations, we track the process by which a given system ends up in an observational dataset and estimate the impact of the binary and pulsar parameters. We fold this selection function into hierarchical population inference and extract the intrinsic astrophysical distribution of pulsars observed with radio timing. We jointly infer the populations of pulsar mass, companion mass, orbital eccentricity, orbital period and pulsar spin period. Selection effects have minimal impact on the mass distributions, but more significantly affect other parameters. The ratio of circular-to-eccentric systems shifts from an observed $1\!:\!1$ to an astrophysical, selection-corrected $2\!:\!1$ ratio, while the true population is shifted toward longer orbital and spin periods than observed.

### [4] [A targeted search for fast radio bursts from magnetars in elliptical galaxies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04118)
*S. Paine,D. R. Lorimer,S. Sirota,G. M. Doskoch,S. Tabassum,M. Flanagan,J. Lituchy,A. Stone,J. W. Kania,M. Bhardwaj,S. Mehta,M. A. McLaughlin,B. Kharel,D. Agarwal,M. P. Surnis*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该论文报告了对五个本地大质量椭圆星系进行的定向高灵敏度 L 波段 FRB 搜索，在 M49 方向上发现 FRB 20240817A，但经前景电子柱校正后判定其为背景事件。无明确的主持星系定位暴发，使作者对星系系统内单个活跃源的内在暴发率给出联合上限 $R_0 < 0.028~\rm{hr}^{-1}$，并推断动力学星团内磁星形成效率较低。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基于附近巨椭圆星系相伴的球状星团是探测来自磁星的快速射电暴的理想环境的预测，作者尝试在五个本地大质量椭圆星系中进行高灵敏度定向 FRB 搜索，以寻找可能的磁星射电暴并检验磁星形成的效率。

Method: 利用绿岸望远镜（GBT）上的 GREENBURST 单脉冲搜索流程和 VEGAS 后端，在 L 波段（1.4 GHz）对五个本地大质量椭圆星系开展定向高灵敏度 FRB 巡天。采用贝叶斯空间关联框架评估宿主，并量化银河系、室女座星系团内介质及 M49 热气体晕的前景电子柱贡献，考虑观测到的延展球状星团分布比例设置暴发率上限。

Result: 在 M49（NGC 4472）方向发现 FRB 20240817A，其脉冲宽度为 15 ms，信噪比为 13，DM 为 $983~\rm{cm}^{-3}$ pc；贝叶斯框架起初将 M49 标记为主持星系，但考虑约 $\sim300-400~\rm{cm}^{-3}$ pc 的前景电子柱贡献后强烈不支持物理关联，判定其为背景事件。另在 M49 方向探测到 DM 为 $150-160~\rm{cm}^{-3}$ pc 的三个低显著性重复脉冲簇。给出了内在暴发率联合上限 $R_0 < 0.028~\rm{hr}^{-1}$ 及 M49 综合速率上限 $<1.0$~hr$^{-1}$，降低了此前上限。

Conclusion: 作者明确得出结论，FRB 20240817A 是背景事件，与 M49 无物理关联；缺乏明确的主持星系定位暴发使观测将内在磁星暴发率上限降至 $R_0 < 0.028~\rm{hr}^{-1}$，并暗示动力学星团内磁星形成效率低于此前假设。根据摘要无法判断这些结果是否可直接迁移至其他磁星或一般 FRB 的物理模型。

Abstract: Motivated by predictions that globular clusters associated with nearby giant elliptical galaxies are prime environments for detecting fast radio bursts (FRBs) from magnetars, we report on a targeted high-sensitivity search across five local massive elliptical galaxies. We utilized the GREENBURST single-pulse search pipeline and the VEGAS backend on the Green Bank Telescope (GBT) to conduct an L-band (1.4 GHz) survey for FRBs in each target environment. We discovered FRB 20240817A in the direction of M49 (NGC 4472) which has a pulse width of 15 ms, a signal-to-noise ratio of 13, and a dispersion measure (DM) of $983~\rm{cm}^{-3}$ pc. Although a Bayesian spatial association framework initially flags M49 as the host, carefully accounting for the foreground electron column contributions from the Milky Way and both the Virgo intracluster medium and M49's hot gaseous halo ($\sim300-400~\rm{cm}^{-3}$ pc combined) strongly disfavours a physical connection. We conclude that FRB 20240817A is a background event. We also identified a repeating cluster of three low-significance pulses from the direction of M49 at a physically plausible DM of $150-160~\rm{cm}^{-3}$ pc. Accounting for the fraction of each target's extended globular cluster distribution observed, the absence of unambiguous, host-localized bursts allows us to place a joint upper limit on the intrinsic burst rate of a single active source within these systems of $R_0 < 0.028~\rm{hr}^{-1}$ (and an integrated rate limit of $<1.0$~hr$^{-1}$ for M49), lowering previous upper limits and suggesting that magnetar formation within dynamical clusters is less efficient than previously assumed.

### [5] [A fast, wide-field, and real-time imaging prototype for large aperture arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03027)
*Thyagarajan, Nithyanandan,Thekkeppattu, Jishnu,Humphrey, David*

Main category: scix

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探测FRB和引力波并合电磁对应体等快速瞬变源需要亚毫秒级的实时处理与即时定位。然而传统相关器架构以$\mathcal{O}(N^2)$缩放，在数千至数万单元的大型阵列上带来计算、带宽和功耗挑战，制约视场、分辨率和观测周期，限制瞬变发现潜力，因此需要寻求替代方案。

Method: 摘要未说明具体观测数据；方法上提出采用E场并行成像“相关器”（EPIC）直接对全视场成像，避免成对相关。研究对比传统相关器的$\mathcal{O}(N^2)$缩放与EPIC的$\mathcal{O}(N \log N)$缩放，指出EPIC已在LWA Sevilleta站用GPU演示，但受内存带宽限制。作者正在开发基于FPGA的分级实现，利用高度并行FFT流水线，并以初步结果从可扩展性角度评估其性能。

Conclusion: 作者认为，通过避免显式成对相关并使用FPGA的高度并行FFT流水线，EPIC原型有望在大型孔径阵列上以较低能耗实现实时、宽视场成像，从而避免传统GPU实现的内存带宽瓶颈。据此，该方案可能适用于SKA-Low和中频孔径阵列等下一代仪器。根据摘要无法判断该实现与脉冲星、磁星计时或FRB物理的直接关联。

Abstract: Real-time processing on sub-millisecond timescales is essential for detecting fast astrophysical transients such as fast radio bursts (FRBs) and prompt electromagnetic counterparts to gravitational-wave mergers. Immediate localisation enables rapid multi-wavelength follow-up and maximises scientific return. As a result, real-time capability has become a key requirement for modern wide-field aperture arrays, which are increasingly being deployed at scales of thousands to tens of thousands of antenna elements. However, traditional correlator architectures scale as $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$, creating significant computational, bandwidth, and power challenges for large-$N$ arrays. These constraints often limit field of view, angular resolution, or observing duty cycle, ultimately reducing transient discovery potential. Direct imaging of the full field of view using E-field Parallel Imaging ``Correlator'' (EPIC) offers a promising alternative by avoiding the explicit formation of pairwise correlations. We, therefore, are developing a prototype implementation of EPIC using Field Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGA) that exploits highly parallel FFT pipelines to improve computational efficiency and reduce power consumption. Using preliminary results, we evaluate its performance particularly from the viewpoint of scalability, and discuss its suitability for next-generation instruments including SKA-Low and mid-frequency aperture arrays.
