### [1] [Detection of hard X-ray pulsations in non-ULX X-ray pulsar 3X J0042 in M31](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10699)

### [2] [Evidence for the binary nature of the long-period radio transient ASKAP/DART J1832-0911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11068)

  <summary>Details</summary>

However, our model makes testable predictions.

### [3] [Nonbirefringent model of orthogonal polarization modes in radio pulsars - New view on S swing and mode structure in pulsar beam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11220)
*J. Dyks,A. Frankowski*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 该文提出基于射电信号非相干叠加的二维偏振模型，仅涉及单一固有模式（如O模式），却能自然产生两个正交偏振轨迹、平均轮廓中三重形式的偏振模式分离以及模式在纬度上的位移，从而表明解释两个正交偏振轨迹可能并不需要两个固有模式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 射电脉冲星信号中观测到的两个正交偏振模式长期以来被归因于强磁化等离子体中波振荡的固有模式；但近来已表明，对于产生非相干叠加偏振信号的扩展发射区域，它们很容易出现，这促使作者重新审视双模式解释的必要性。

Method: 摘要未说明具体的观测、数据、仪器、频率或时间跨度。作者提出一个基于射电信号非相干叠加的二维偏振模型；该模型只涉及单一固有模式（例如O模式），并将其应用于扩展发射区域以及锥形发射区域情形，以考察所得到的正交偏振轨迹与平均轮廓中的模式结构。

Result: 该单固有模式的非相干叠加模型导致两个正交偏振轨迹出现，并自然产生平均轮廓中偏振模式分离的三重形式，即中央模式两侧各被另一个模式环绕，还产生先前从波束成像推断出的模式在纬度上的位移。在锥形发射区域情况下，建模得到的偏振倾向于模仿旋转矢量模型（RVM）的一般偏振特性，但其原因是发射区域的对称性，而非通常的偶极磁场方位角投影；由此得到的RVM参数揭示的是发射区域的几何结构，而非偶极磁场的几何结构。

Conclusion: 作者认为，这些结果强烈支持非双折射模式效应在射电脉冲星轮廓中的关键作用，并指出可能并不需要两个固有模式来解释对两个正交偏振轨迹的观测。根据摘要无法判断该模型与磁星、快速射电暴（FRB）、计时、自转减慢力矩变化等具体课题的联系。

Abstract: Two orthogonal polarization modes observed in radio pulsar signals have long been attributed to proper modes of wave oscillation in strongly magnetized plasma. Yet it has been shown recently that they show up readily for extended emission regions that produce incoherently-superposed polarization signal. In this paper we present a two-dimensional polarization model based on incoherent superposition of radio signals. The model involves a single proper mode, say the O mode, but leads to the appearance of two orthogonal polarization tracks and naturally produces the triple form of polarization mode segregation in averaged profiles (central mode flanked on boths sides by another mode), as well as the displacement of modes in latitude, previously inferred from beam mapping. In the case of conal emission regions, the modelled polarization tends to mimic general polarization properties of the rotating vector model (RVM). However, the reason for this is the symmetry of the emission region - not the usual projection of dipolar magnetic azimuths. Thus the emerging RVM parameters reveal geometry of the emission region, not of the dipolar magnetic field. The results strongly support the vital role of nonbirefringent modal effects in radio pulsar profiles. Two proper modes may not be needed to explain observations of two orthogonal polarization tracks.
