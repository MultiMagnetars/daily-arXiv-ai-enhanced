### [1] [Spectral and Polarization Properties of Coherent Radio Emission from Relativistic Magnetized Pair Plasma Shocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30494)
*Yuanhong Qu,Joonas Nättilä,Lorenzo Sironi*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 三维粒子模拟表明，相对论磁化正负电子对等离子体激波中的同步加速器微波激射不稳定性能够产生带宽和高线偏振度与部分非重复 FRB 观测相符的辐射，并可能成为区分激波供能 FRB 的检验特征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FRB 的辐射机制仍是未解问题；相对论磁化激波中的同步加速器微波激射不稳定性虽是致密天体磁层之外 FRB 的主要候选机制，但其光谱和偏振预言此前尚未被系统探索。该研究即针对这一空白，检验该机制的光谱与偏振特征。

Method: 采用三维粒子模拟（particle-in-cell simulations）研究相对论磁化正负电子对等离子体激波，参数为上游磁化参数 $σ> 1$ 和体洛伦兹因子 $γ_0 = 10$；假设平面平行激波几何，忽略辐射冷却；对任意视线方向计算前驱微波激射辐射的光谱和偏振，并假设球对称激波给出分数带宽。其余观测细节摘要未说明。

Result: 对于与激波传播方向对齐的轴上观测者，X 模主导辐射；O 模功率在离轴视角下增加，且主要与平行于上游背景磁场的电流相关。假设球对称激波，$σ=3$ 和 $6$ 时半高全宽分数带宽分别为 $Δν/ν_0\simeq0.57$ 和 $0.82$。高度磁化激波中内禀辐射带宽贡献大部分光谱宽度，高纬度贡献提供额外展宽。积分辐射在光谱峰附近保持高度线偏振，偏振角几乎不变，归因于 X 模主导。

Conclusion: 作者认为，同步加速器微波激射辐射能产生带宽和高线偏振度与某些非重复 FRB 观测相符的光谱，并可能提供可检验的特征，用于区分激波供能的 FRB 与其他辐射机制。该解释是否适用于具体 FRB 样本或磁星—FRB 联系，根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: Fast radio bursts (FRBs) are millisecond-duration radio transients whose emission mechanism remains an open question. The so-called synchrotron maser instability in relativistic magnetized shocks is a leading candidate for FRBs produced outside the compact object magnetosphere, yet its spectral and polarization predictions have not been systematically explored. We present three-dimensional particle-in-cell simulations of relativistic magnetized pair plasma shocks with upstream magnetization $σ> 1$ and bulk Lorentz factor $γ_0 = 10$, assuming a plane-parallel shock geometry and neglecting radiative cooling, and compute the spectra and polarization of the precursor maser emission for an arbitrary line of sight. For on-axis observers aligned with the shock-propagation direction, the X-mode dominates the emission. The O-mode power increases at off-axis viewing angles and is primarily associated with currents parallel to the upstream background magnetic field. Assuming a spherical shock, we find full-width-at-half-maximum fractional bandwidths of $Δν/ν_0\simeq0.57$ and $0.82$ for $σ=3$ and $6$, respectively. For highly magnetized shocks, the intrinsic emission bandwidth accounts for most of the spectral width, while high-latitude contributions provide additional broadening. The integrated emission remains highly linearly polarized near the spectral peak, with a nearly constant polarization angle due to the dominance of the X-mode. Our results suggest that synchrotron maser emission can produce spectra with bandwidths and high linear polarization degrees compatible with observations of some non-repeating FRBs, and may provide testable signatures for distinguishing shock-powered FRBs from other emission mechanisms.

### [2] [Tracing the Host-Galaxy Environments of FRBs with a New Sample of Localized Sources Detected with the DSA-110](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30616)
*Frank M. Verdi,Vikram Ravi,Myles B. Sherman,Jakob T. Faber,Stella Koch Ocker,Kritti Sharma,Casey J. Law,Gregg Hallinan,Kaitlyn Shin,Liam Connor*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 利用DSA-110探测并定位于宿主星系的43个已知红移FRB样本，发现宿主星系对DM和RM的贡献典型地很大且显著相关，且宿主星系ISM中的密度涨落可能是河外散射的主要来源；结果支持FRB与HII区相关联，并倾向支持核心坍缩超新星形成的磁星作为FRB来源。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 刻画FRB的源环境有助于理解其宿主星系ISM性质与起源。然而，需要一批已被定位于具有光谱红移宿主星系的FRB样本来测量宿主星系对DM和RM的贡献，并检验河外散射来源及FRB与HII区、前身星群体的关系。

Method: 分析由深度同步阵列（DSA-110）探测到并被定位于具有光谱红移宿主星系的24个FRB样本，其中4个与红移1附近或超过红移1的星系相关联，部分为首次发表；将其与先前已分析的DSA-110源合并，得到43个已知红移FRB样本，用于测量宿主星系对DM和RM的贡献，并识别具有可探测散射的FRB子集，结合对部分暴的双屏模型建模；还将FRB宿主星系性质与脉冲星及年轻和年老前身星群体的模拟进行比较。

Result: 24个新分析的FRB已被定位于具有光谱红移的宿主星系，其中4个被确信地与红移1附近或超过红移1的星系相关联，若干为首次发表；结合先前源得到43个已知红移的DSA-110 FRB样本。宿主星系对DM和RM的贡献典型地很大且显著相关；一部分FRB具有可探测散射，其散射时标和推断的宿主DM值与为银河系脉冲星建立的经验关系大致一致；结合部分暴的双屏模型建模，结果表明宿主星系ISM内的密度涨落是河外散射的主要来源。

Conclusion: 作者认为，典型地很大且相关的宿主星系DM和RM贡献表明大多数FRB位于其宿主星系ISM中过密且磁化的区域；宿主星系ISM内的密度涨落是河外散射的主要来源；FRB宿主星系性质与脉冲星以及年轻和年老前身星群体模拟的比较支持FRB在其整个辐射寿命期间优先形成并保持与HII区相关联，从而倾向支持核心坍缩超新星中形成的磁星作为FRB的来源。关于该结论对脉冲星、磁星、单脉冲、模式转换或计时等具体课题的可迁移性，根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: We characterize the source environments of a sample of fast radio bursts (FRBs) detected with the Deep Synoptic Array (DSA-110). We present new analyses of a sample of 24 FRBs which have been localized to host galaxies with spectroscopic redshifts. Four of these bursts have been confidently associated with galaxies near or beyond redshift 1, and several are published here for the first time. Combining these new bursts with previously analyzed DSA-110 sources, we use a sample of 43 DSA-110 FRBs with known redshifts to measure host-galaxy contributions to dispersion measure (DM) and Faraday rotation measure (RM). We find characteristically large and significantly correlated host-galaxy contributions to DM and RM, suggesting that most FRBs reside in overdense, magnetized regions within their host ISM. Additionally, we identify a subset of FRBs with detectable scattering, and show that their scattering timescales and inferred host DM values are broadly consistent with an empirical relation established for Galactic pulsars. Combined with two-screen modeling of some bursts, our results indicate that density fluctuations within the host-galaxy ISM are the dominant source of extragalactic scattering. Comparisons of FRB host-galaxy properties with pulsars and simulations of young and old progenitor populations support a scenario in which FRBs preferentially form and remain associated with HII regions throughout their emission lifetime, favoring magnetars formed in core-collapse supernovae as the sources of FRBs.

### [3] [4FHL: The Fourth Catalog of Hard Fermi-LAT Sources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31457)
*The Fermi-LAT Collaboration*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 4FHL星表基于Fermi-LAT 16年观测，在50 GeV-2 TeV探测到673个源，灵敏度与定位精度较2FHL提高约$\sim$2倍，是迄今最深的50 GeV以上全天巡天。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在50 GeV以上高能区间对全天源进行系统性编目，有助于连接空间基伽马射线观测与地面天文台探测的TeV区间，并支持更详细的源族群研究及后续观测目标的选取。

Method: 利用费米伽马射线空间望远镜上的费米大面积探测器（LAT）在16年观测中探测50 GeV以上的源，覆盖50 GeV-2 TeV能量范围，共编制673个天体，并与2FHL星表比较灵敏度与定位精度。摘要未说明更具体的分析流程、统计检验或仪器细节。

Result: 4FHL包含50 GeV-2 TeV范围内673个源；灵敏度和定位精度较2FHL提高约$\sim$2倍，探测数量几乎翻倍。84.7\%的源与河外对应体关联，主要为BL Lac天体，其中36个红移$z>1$；银河系内源占10\%，主要是脉冲星风星云和超新星遗迹；$\sim$5\%未被关联或分类不确定；二十个源在先前LAT星表中无对应体。

Conclusion: 4FHL星表提供了迄今最深远的50 GeV以上全天巡天，在高能区间为空间基伽马射线观测与当前和未来地面天文台所探测的TeV区间之间提供基础性联系，并可指导未来观测，包括为切伦科夫望远镜后续观测提供新的潜在目标。

Abstract: We present a catalog of sources detected above 50 GeV by the Fermi Large Area Telescope (LAT) on board the Fermi Gamma-ray Space Telescope using 16 years of observations. This is the Fourth Catalog of Hard Fermi-LAT Sources (4FHL), containing 673 objects detected in the 50 GeV-2 TeV energy range. The sensitivity and localization are improved by factors of $\sim$2 relative to the previous LAT catalog at the same energies (i.e. the 2FHL catalog). The increased photon statistics, nearly doubling the number of detections relative to 2FHL, enables more detailed population studies, including the identification of fainter sources detected above 50 GeV. The majority of the sources (84.7\%) are associated with extragalactic counterparts, primarily BL Lacertae objects, including 36 objects at redshift $z>1$. Galactic sources account for 10\% of the sample, mainly pulsar wind nebulae and supernova remnants, while $\sim$5\% remain unassociated or of uncertain classification. Twenty sources are reported with no counterpart in previous LAT catalogs. The 4FHL catalog provides the deepest all-sky survey above 50 GeV to date. By focusing on this high-energy regime, it provides a fundamental link between space-based gamma-ray observations and the TeV range probed by current and future ground-based observatories. It serves as a useful reference for guiding future observations, including new potential targets for follow-up with Cherenkov telescopes.

### [4] [Probing the cosmic web with fast radio bursts: II. Scintillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31106)
*Lapiner, Sharon,Mandelker, Nir,Beniamini, Paz,Oh, S. Peng*

Main category: scix

TL;DR: 该文探讨宇宙网天体（CWOs）中冷而致密的小尺度云滴如何通过抑制银河系星际介质闪烁来影响FRB传播，并提出未来FRB样本可据此约束这些云滴的密度、大小、体积填充因子与湍流性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究旨在利用FRB射电波传播探测宇宙网天体（片层、纤维结构与晕的CGM）中的小尺度冷气体结构，填补对这些多相云滴及其冷气体性质缺乏观测约束的空白；银河系星际介质闪烁被视为有前景的探针。

Method: 摘要描述了理论建模与估计：考虑冲击加热的宇宙片层、纤维结构及晕CGM中多相气体经冷却与碎裂形成冷云滴，分析其对FRB流量闪烁图案的影响，并提出以银河系星际介质闪烁受抑制作为探针；还估计了预期遇到相关CWOs的FRB比例，并表示为平均河外色散量的函数。具体观测、仪器、频段、样本与统计方法摘要未说明。

Result: 摘要指出，遇到带有冷云滴的高温纤维结构或片层的概率相当小且通常局限于高红移，但高FRB发生率仍可能通过沿稀有视线方向的闪烁实现探测。纤维结构与片层可分别抑制来自 $z_\mathrm{S}\gtrsim2$ 和 $3$ 处源的MW闪烁；CGM中冷且湍流的云滴可由红移低至 $z_\mathrm{S}\sim0.5$ 的源探测，并将体积填充因子约束到极低值，例如 $z_\mathrm{S}\gtrsim2$ 时 $f_\mathrm{v}\gtrsim10^{-5}$。

Conclusion: 作者认为，CWO等离子体屏对银河系星际介质闪烁的抑制可对CWOs的冷气体性质施加限制，未来FRB样本有望将这些限制映射为对CWOs中云滴存在及其密度、大小、$f_\mathrm{v}$ 和湍流性质的约束。根据摘要无法判断该探针的实际观测可行性或与其他FRB、脉冲星、磁星解释的具体联系。

Abstract: We consider the formation of multiphase gas in cosmic web objects (CWOs) through cooling and fragmentation within the shock-heated medium of cosmic sheets and filaments, and the circumgalactic medium (CGM) of haloes. This leads to dense, cool ($\sim10^{4}\,\mathrm{K}$), small-scale cloudlets in pressure equilibrium with their surroundings. Such ionized overdensities can affect the propagation of radio waves emitted by fast radio bursts (FRBs), allowing us to probe the small-scale structure in CWOs by examining their impact on the scintillation pattern of the observed FRB flux. While we find that the probability of encountering a high-temperature filament or sheet with cold cloudlets is rather small and usually confined to high redshifts, the expected high rates of FRBs may still permit detection through scintillation along rare sightlines. We suggest scintillation from the Milky Way (MW) interstellar medium as a promising probe: CWO plasma screens can suppress it, placing constraints on their cold-gas properties. Filaments and sheets can suppress MW scintillation from sources at $z_\mathrm{S}\gtrsim2$ and $3$, respectively, while cold and turbulent cloudlets in the CGM can be probed by sources at redshifts as low as $z_\mathrm{S}\sim0.5$, constraining remarkably low volume-filling fractions, $f_\mathrm{v}$ (e.g. $f_\mathrm{v}\gtrsim10^{-5}$ for $z_\mathrm{S}\gtrsim2$). To provide testable predictions, we estimate the fraction of FRBs expected to encounter CWOs that may suppress MW scintillation below a given frequency, and express this as a function of the average extragalactic dispersion measure. Future FRB samples could map these constraints onto bounds on the existence and properties of cloudlets in CWOs, such as their density, size, $f_\mathrm{v}$, and turbulence.
