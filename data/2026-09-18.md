Motivation: 中子星半径限制可揭示其内部冷的致密物质性质，且此前合成 NICER 脉冲波形数据研究已显示半径推断对若干建模系统效应稳健；本文关注的空白是：若假设错误的大气成分（氢与氦不匹配），会对半径推断造成何种后果。

Even in these cases, the Bayesian evidence consistently identifies the correct atmospheric model. Our findings reinforce the importance of using the Bayesian evidence for model comparison and goodness-of-fit tests.

These include the tidal deformation of the stars due to their respective companion, but for spinning neutron stars also include the spin-induced quadrupole moment of the stars. Parameter estimation using the spin-extended relation reduces biases in recovered intrinsic parameters in some cases, though biases from mass-spin degeneracies dominate largely when a population is considered. These results provide a more accurate description of the spin-induced quadrupole moment for spinning neutron star binaries, relevant for waveform modelling with third-generation gravitational-wave detectors.
