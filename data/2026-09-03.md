Motivation: 富气体环境中的超大质量双黑洞是脉冲星计时阵列和未来天基引力波干涉仪的重要多信使源，其电磁辐射由非线性等离子体动力学主导并带有轨道变异性。由于活动星系核本质随机，需要能连接大尺度环双星流与黑洞的预测模型；而先前模拟多集中于较小间距和较低自转，本研究针对更大间距与高自转情形展开。

Method: 采用三维广义相对论磁流体动力学模拟，模拟内容为松弛、磁化的环双星盘吸积到等质量双黑洞上，间距为$30$个引力半径、无量纲自转$χ=0.9$。通过分析磁通量从环双星盘到视界的输运过程，并比较空腔内高效与低效冷却两种迷你盘热力学情况。<br>摘要未说明具体观测频率、仪器或时间跨度。

Result: 在此间距下迷你盘为持续性质量库，但表现出由偏心环双星盘和迷你盘间摆动气体主导的显著振幅调制。磁通量从环双星盘输运到视界，引发出能量提取效率达$\approx 40\%$的强大双喷流。贯穿视界的磁通量和喷流光度在两黑洞间交替，形成开-关双喷流状态；宽阔喷流漏斗在双星上方相互作用形成有利于磁重联的持续性电流片。更热的迷你盘质量更小、相干周期性更弱，尽管贯穿视界磁通量相当，喷流光度却更低。

Conclusion: 作者结论：高自转、大间距的吸积双黑洞可通过磁通输运高效提取能量并产生强大的开-关交替双喷流状态，漏斗相互作用形成的电流片有利于磁重联。迷你盘热力学状态会调节喷流亮度与相干周期性：更热的迷你盘喷射出光度较低的喷流。<br>对脉冲星/磁星/FRB的转移性，根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: Supermassive binary black holes embedded in gas-rich environments are promising multi-messenger sources for pulsar timing arrays and future space-borne gravitational-wave interferometers. Their electromagnetic emission is governed by nonlinear plasma dynamics around the binary and is expected to inherit variability associated with the orbital motion thereof. Because active galactic nuclei are intrinsically stochastic, identifying robust binary signatures requires predictive models that connect large-scale circumbinary flows to the black holes. Previous relativistic simulations of accreting binaries have mostly focused on smaller separations and lower spins. We perform three-dimensional general relativistic magnetohydrodynamic simulations of a relaxed, magnetized circumbinary disk accreting onto an equal-mass binary black hole with a separation of $30$ gravitational radii and dimensionless spin $χ=0.9$. At these separations, the mini-disks around each black hole are persistent mass reservoirs but still show pronounced amplitude modulations governed by the eccentric circumbinary disk and the sloshing gas between the mini-disks. We analyze how the magnetic flux is transported from the circumbinary disk to the horizons, launching powerful dual jets with energy extraction efficiencies reaching $\approx 40\%$. The horizon-threading flux and jet luminosity alternate between the two black holes, producing an on-off dual-jet state. The wide jet funnels interact above the binary and form a persistent current sheet favorable to reconnection. We explore the influence of mini-disk thermodynamics, comparing efficient and inefficient cooling inside the cavity. Hotter mini-disks are less massive, exhibit weaker coherent periodicity, and launch less luminous jets despite comparable horizon-threading magnetic flux.
