### [3] [Angular structure of finite-field vacuum birefringence in magnetars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38573)
*Shahram Abbassi,S. R. Valluri*

Main category: astro-ph.HE

TL;DR: 在局域 Heisenberg--Euler 理论中，正双折射分支上常用的 $\sin^2θ$ 因子化形式会系统性高估偏离垂直传播时的精确单圈劈裂，该相对修正最靠近磁场方向处最大；在磁星中心偶极应用中对中等磁场为亚百分比，对最强源可达百分之几。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 强磁场中的真空双折射常以垂直传播的有限场劈裂乘以 $\sin^2θ$ 角度因子来建模，但摘要指出这种分离仅在领头响应阶精确。此前研究考察了双折射的场强依赖性，本文则针对其角度依赖性，追问完整单圈折射率在偏离垂直传播时的行为与因子化形式的偏差。

Method: 在局域 Heisenberg--Euler 理论中解析考察完整单圈折射率的角度依赖性；给出紧凑解析展开，刻画修正的符号、标度与角度位置，覆盖 $B/B_{\rm cr}\le100$ 的磁星相关范围；并用一项独立的固有时（proper-time）计算验证潜在单圈系数；此外在中心偶极情形下考察该修正。观测仪器、频段与时间跨度摘要未说明。

Conclusion: 作者将结果表述为分离了有限场振幅修正与角度修正，并为强磁化环境中的精密偏振传播提供了受控的解析基准。该工作是否可直接用于脉冲星、磁星单脉冲、脉冲轮廓演化、模式转换、计时、自转减慢力矩变化、偏振、射电谱、暂现射电辐射或磁星与 FRB 的联系，根据摘要无法判断。

That separation is exact only at leading response order. Continuing our previous study of the field-strength dependence of the birefringence, we examine the angular dependence of the full one-loop refractive indices in the local Heisenberg--Euler theory. We show analytically that, on the positive-birefringence branch, the usual factorized form systematically overestimates the exact splitting away from perpendicular propagation. The relative correction is largest close to the magnetic-field direction, where the absolute birefringence itself vanishes. Compact analytic expansions expose the sign, scaling, and angular location of the correction throughout the magnetar-relevant range $B/B_{\rm cr}\le100$, while an independent proper-time calculation verifies the underlying one-loop coefficients. In centred-dipole applications the correction is sub-percent for moderate magnetar fields but reaches several percent for the strongest sources; surface averaging and radial phase accumulation reduce its physical weight as the field decreases away from the stellar surface. The result separates finite-field amplitude and angular corrections and provides controlled analytic benchmarks for precision polarization transport in strongly magnetized environments.

Abstract: Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs) are spatially and temporally compact sources at cosmological distances. While current burst rates and low repeating-FRB fractions make a detection challenging in the immediate term, upcoming high-volume surveys and next-generation arrays (such as the SKA Phase 2) will bring the unambiguous detection of extragalactic exoplanets across cosmological redshifts into possibility.

Abstract: Pulsar timing arrays detect signals from dark matter substructure, but a nanohertz gravitational-wave background (GWB) severely degrades their sensitivity.
