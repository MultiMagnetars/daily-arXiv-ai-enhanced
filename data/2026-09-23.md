Main category: scix

TL;DR: 该研究首次用完全广义相对论方法计算具有暗能量核心的中子星的非径向 $f$-模振荡，发现暗能量使状态方程软化、最大质量降低，且所预测的特征引力波应变落在未来第三代引力波探测器灵敏度波段内。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 论文旨在研究暗能量核心如何通过引力效应影响中子星的振荡与结构。其科学背景是，中子星内部可能存在暗能量核心，而此类核心对非径向 $f$-模振荡、潮汐形变及引力波辐射的影响此前尚未得到研究；摘要明确指出这是首次开展此类完全广义相对论处理的研究。

Method: 摘要说明采用完全广义相对论处理，核物质使用相对论平均场模型，暗能量采用类修正 Chaplygin 流体方案，并改变暗能量状态方程参数。计算内容包括恒星结构、$f$-模振荡、潮汐形变以及引力波能量与应变；并将质量-半径结果与脉冲星 PSR J0030+0451、PSR J0740+6620 的观测约束比较，将 $f$-模频率与潮汐形变的相关性与 GW170817、GW190814 的观测约束比较。具体观测频段、时间跨度与样本量摘要未说明。

Conclusion: 作者结论是：暗能量核心会软化状态方程、降低最大质量，并显著改变恒星结构与振荡特性；计算结果与大质量脉冲星、GW170817 和 GW190814 的观测约束相符。归一化振荡能量分布的近通用性及较高质量位形的系统性 $f$-模改变，可能使未来第三代引力波探测器有机会探测相关信号。对于该研究是否可推广到脉冲星单脉冲、模式转换、计时、射电偏振或磁星—快速射电暴联系，摘要未说明或根据摘要无法判断。

Abstract: We investigate, for the first time, the non-radial $f$-mode oscillations of neutron stars (NSs) with a dark energy (DE) core utilising a fully general relativistic treatment. We construct the stellar profile by considering a relativistic mean field model for nuclear matter and a modified Chaplygin fluid like prescription for DE. We compute the stellar structure, $f$-mode oscillations, tidal deformability, and gravitational wave (GW) energy and strain of the NS with DE core by varying the DE equation of state (EoS) parameters. Our study reveals that the inclusion of DE softens EoS and reduces the maximum mass compared to the pure neutron star. The obtained mass-radius values are consistent with observational constraints from massive pulsars such as PSR J0030+0451 and PSR J0740+6620. We find that the extent of the DE core, determined by the transition density, plays a crucial role in modifying the stellar structure and oscillation properties, with lower transition densities producing appreciable changes over a wider mass range. The $f$-mode frequencies and damping times exhibit systematic modifications, for higher mass configurations, while the correlation between the $f$-mode frequency and tidal deformability remains consistent with observational constraints from GW170817 and GW190814. Further, the normalised oscillation energy distribution remains nearly universal, with only minimal deviations for the higher mass configurations. Finally, we estimate the characteristic GW strain associated with the $f$-mode oscillations and find that the predicted signals lie within the sensitivity band of future third generation GW detectors.
